## Repair summary

The C10 completion-result filter at
`apps/vscode/src/sdk/sdk-session-event-coordinator.ts:514-622` was
narrowed from the predecessor ACT-CLINEMM-BACKGROUND-COMMAND-TERMINAL-PRESENTATION-ARBITRATION01's
over-broad aggregate predicate (`activeNotifyCount > 0 ||
pendingPromptsKnown > 0 || pendingPromptAuthorityUnknown`) to a
per-job ownership-aware predicate.

The narrow predicate is:
  suppress(C) IFF owned(C) is defined AND hasActiveNotify(owned(C)) == true

where `owned(C)` is the per-turn ownership hint
(`MessageTranslatorState.launchedBackgroundJobIds`) populated at the
SAME seam as `BackgroundNotifyCoordinator.registerMarker` (the C9 ->
marker seam at `vscode-run-commands-tool.ts:755-816`).

The lookup is exact: `BackgroundNotifyCoordinator.hasActiveNotify(jobId)`
is a constant-time `notificationMarkers.has(jobId)` against the
underlying `Map<string, NotificationMarker>`. No new state, no new
protocol field, no new persistence, no new wire surface.
