ACT-CLINEMM-EXTENSION-HOST-TERMINATION-AUTHORITY01 -- Conservation

The termination witness is DEFAULT_OFF (CLINEMM_DIAG_TERMINATION_AUTHORITY
unset or non-truthy keeps state=disabled). When disabled:

TATRM-CONSERVE-01  disabled-zero-semantic-delta
                   - installTerminationAuthorityWitness() returns
                     without installing ANY listeners on process.
                   - No writers, no readFileSync, no FS IO.
                   - __resetTerminationAuthorityForTests() can be
                     called at any time; safe to leave wired.
                   - State accessor always reports "disabled"; the
                     capturedId is undefined.

The DEFAULT_OFF state was verified by:

  - The vitest test suite TATRM-CONSERVE-01 counts listeners on
    process.before/after calling install(); asserts delta=0.
  - test:unit (bun:test runner) over 1168 tests in apps/vscode
    returned 0 failures -- the existing ACTs (CCARD, BTCONT, TQCB,
    ALLOCAUTH, CPUCAP, SLAC, EHLOOP, ...) are unaffected.
