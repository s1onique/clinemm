MECHANISM — operation 1
  predecessor pattern:
    ("?)((?:[A-Za-z0-9_.-]*(?:password|...)[A-Za-z0-9_.-]*)\1\s*[:=]\s*)(VALUE)
  The leading [A-Za-z0-9_.-]* is greedy and NOTHING anchors the START of the key
  run. The engine attempts the match at EVERY offset inside the run; at each one it
  consumes the run and backtracks through the run's characters looking for the
  secret name, the closing quote, the :/= and a value. On a body with no match,
  every attempt fails after a full traversal of the run.
  cost = O(run length) per offset x O(run length) offsets = O(n^2)

MECHANISM — operation 3
  predecessor pattern:
    -----BEGIN [A-Z ]*PRIVATE KEY-----[\s\S]*?-----END [A-Z ]*PRIVATE KEY-----
  The LAZY [\s\S]*? span means: for EACH BEGIN marker, rescan forward to the end of
  the input looking for an END marker.
  cost = O(markers x body length)

A CLAIM NOT MADE
  EXPONENTIAL COMPLEXITY IS NOT CLAIMED. Both behaviours are SUPERLINEAR
  (quadratic), established by the 2x/4x growth measurements. Superlinear
  pathological behaviour is sufficient to justify the repair.
