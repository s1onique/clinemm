   under CLINEMM_EXPERIMENTAL_SANDBOX=seatbelt +
   CLINEMM_SAFE_YOLO_NETWORK=allow, the SSH transport reached the

   NETWORK_EGRESS_PROCEEDED is real on a real ClineMM sandboxed
   execution path. The original D/A/O host-ablation causality is
   owned by ACT-CLINEMM-SEATBELT-NETWORK-EGRESS-RECON01's §4
   evidence (under probeSeatbeltAvailability() === true); the two
   are complementary, not redundant.
2. Had network egress still been broken, the failure would have been
   EPERM at connect() (no "Load key" / "Permission denied" lines).
   It was not — the load-bearing observation is the key-file EPERM
   AFTER the transport succeeded.
3. The raw-key denial is BY DESIGN: ~/.ssh/id_rsa is in the V1
   curated deny list emitted by resolveSafeYoloSensitiveReadDenials()

   `(deny file-read* (subpath "<canonical ~/.ssh/id_rsa>"))`.
4. The gap is a MISSING PRODUCT CAPABILITY: there is no rule in the
   SBPL generator today for AF_UNIX / ssh-agent authority, and no
   reintroduction of SSH_AUTH_SOCK in the env. This is the new
   "narrowly-scoped ssh-agent authority" capability proposed by §5
   of ACT-CLINEMM-SEATBELT-SSH-AGENT-AUTHORITY-RECON01.
