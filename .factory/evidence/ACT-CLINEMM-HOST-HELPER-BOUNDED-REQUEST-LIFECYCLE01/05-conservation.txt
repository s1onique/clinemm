## TRANSPORT_TIMEOUT != AUTHORIZATION_FAILURE
A timed-out frame never reaches parse_envelope and never reaches method dispatch,
so it cannot allocate a client token, register a process group, or invoke any
method side effect. T12 proves the capability surface still works after timeouts.
