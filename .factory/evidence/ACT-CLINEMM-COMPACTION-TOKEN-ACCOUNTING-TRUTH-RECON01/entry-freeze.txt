NEW_CAUSAL_CANDIDATE (factory causal reviewer, 2026-09-02)=
  The post-compaction TaskHeader rescaling (apps/vscode/src/shared/
  getApiMetrics.ts:174-225) crosses two semantic baselines:
    - LAST_REQUEST_INPUT (provider-normalized per-request occupancy)
    - WORKING_CONTEXT ESTIMATE shrink ratio (tokensAfter / tokensBefore)
  The displayed header value is their product:
    header = lastRequestInput × (tokensAfter / tokensBefore)
  For the LIVE symptom input (167.1k × 0.04249 ≈ 7.1k), the production
  implementation returns exactly this product. The transformation is
  documented but its semantic validity was not established by ACT-CLINEMM-
  CONTEXT-ACCOUNTING-TRUTH01 (CORRECTION01) — that closure approved the
  rescaling on the basis of "only the ratio carries over" without
  establishing a source-of-truth oracle for what the header SHOULD
  display.
  Resolution requires R0 execution (RED expected). If R0 REDs and
  R1-R3 GREEN, classification is CASE_B.UI; ownership migrates.
