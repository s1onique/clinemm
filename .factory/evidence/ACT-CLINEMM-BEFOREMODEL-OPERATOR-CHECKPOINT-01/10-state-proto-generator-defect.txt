### Impact

The generator also deletes hand-maintained fields and comments, e.g.
`auto_approve_all_toggled = 174`, which the legacy migration at
`src/hosts/vscode/vscode-to-file-migration.ts:289` still reads.

## Resolution used by this ACT

The proto fields were hand-added to the PRISTINE HEAD proto so the
hand-maintained content survives and the file stays well-formed, then the
TypeScript bindings were regenerated from that well-formed proto with
`bun run protos` (exit 0).

The committed proto diff vs pristine HEAD is PURELY ADDITIVE (25 added, 0 removed, 0 modified).
