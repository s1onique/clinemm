PID 14133 happens to be identical because launchd did not need to restart
the helper during the experiment. The ACT explicitly notes:
  "PID may legitimately change, so do not require PID equality."

The fact that it did not change is incidental, not required. What is
required is that the helper remained reachable, which it did.
