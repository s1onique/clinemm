## Explicit anti-overclaims

The contract wording does NOT promise:

  - "all descendants killed"
  - "everything spawned by Cline is killed"
  - "zero leaked processes"
  - "ClineMM guarantees cleanup of session-detached processes"
  - "every completed or cancelled CommandJob ends with the
    primary PGID cleanly gone" (this is exactly the stronger
    universal claim that would overclaim — when the postcondition
    fails, containment_failed is surfaced and the PGID is not
    cleanly cleaned; the contract only says that IF ClineMM
    considers a job cleanly terminal THEN the PGID is gone)

The wording DOES say:

  - ClineMM owns and attempts to clean up each CommandJob's
    primary owned process group when the command completes or
    is cancelled.
  - A CommandJob is considered cleanly terminal only after
    ClineMM establishes that the primary PGID is gone.
  - If that postcondition cannot be established, the job
    terminates as containment_failed and ClineMM surfaces a
    runtime incident.
  - ClineMM does NOT guarantee cleanup of processes that have
    left the CommandJob's primary process group by any mechanism.

The proven invariant (sufficient-condition, not universal) is:

  CLEAN_TERMINAL CommandJob  =>  PRIMARY OWNED PGID GONE

The "by any mechanism" framing deliberately covers `setsid()`,
`setpgid()` / `setpgrp()`, Node `detached:true`, Python
`start_new_session=True`, double-fork daemonization,
`nohup`/`disown` patterns, and any other PGID-move mechanism.
CORRECTION03 of the predecessor contract file retacts the
narrower "only setsid()" framing.
