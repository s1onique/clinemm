TERMINAL_CARD_TRANSITION = DEFERRED_TO_SUCCESSOR_ACT (per CORRECTION02 P0-B scope narrowing)

  - The wire enum is only
    "executed" | "rejected_before_execution" | "backgrounded".
    There is no row-mutation seam today.
  - The narrow product contract is the contract for THIS ACT: the
    row stays "Backgrounded" until something explicitly mutates
    it. The authoritative terminal signal is the ⎇ gauge +
    activeCommandJobs counter (NOT the row pill). Both are driven
    by onBackgroundStateChange and verified by the prior ACT's
    tests.
  - BGCL-09 pins this contract as a regression guard for any
    future ACT that introduces a row-mutation seam.
  - The LIVE operator qualification for THIS ACT observes the
    ⎇ gauge change, NOT the row pill. The card stays
    "Backgrounded" both after user-Cancel and after natural
    command completion. ACT-...-TERMINAL-ROW-MUTATION01 is the
    ONLY successor authorized to introduce the row-mutation
    seam that would make the card transition.

CANCEL_AUTHORITY = command-job-manager.cancel({ jobId })
  is the only cancellation target. The card-level Cancel routes
  the jobId to the gRPC `cancelBackgroundCommand(StringRequest)`
  handler, which forwards to `SdkController.cancelBackgroundCommand(jobId?)`
  -> `vscode-session-host.cancelBackgroundCommand(jobId?)` ->
  `commandJobManager.cancel({ jobId })`. Session-wide cancel is
  preserved for back-compat (empty `value` triggers
  `getActiveJobIds()`).
