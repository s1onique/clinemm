REVIEWER NOTE (paraphrased):

  R7 overclaims mathematical equivalence. The test correctly observes in
  its own commentary that production weight = ceil(N/3) is NOT a
  proportional transformation of JSON weight = N, and therefore the
  threshold-crossing index can differ. But the module-level documentation
  still says:

    cut selection is invariant under monotonic weight transforms

    proving the cut with a deterministic weight proves the cut with
    any monotonic estimator

  These statements are false. A monotonic transformation of individual
  weights does not preserve accumulated-threshold crossings.

  R7 currently proves only:

    ON THIS L1 FIXTURE: JSON-length weighting with threshold 60k and
    production estimator with threshold 20k happen to select the same
    cut.

  It does NOT prove: metric independence, or invariance under arbitrary
  monotonic estimators.
