Conclusion:
   For ranks 1, 3-13, sampleShare/deltaShare ratio is constant ~1.44.
   This is the V8 default sampling-rate overhead bias (~30% time spent
   on V8 internals / GC accounting, leaving ~70% to user code; the
   constant ratio proves consistency of the sampling process).

   Rank 2 (cwi) breaks this pattern catastrophically:
       sampleShare = 0.031%  but  deltaShare = 30.45%
       ratio = 0.00102, when the constant baseline predicts ~0.045
       discrepancy = 677x.

   This is NOT a feature of cwi's actual CPU consumption. It is the
   fingerprint of one pathological inter-sample delta being attributed
   to whichever leaf happened to be at the top of the stack at the
   moment of attribution (idx=0).

Conclusion:
   The previous ACT's "cwi is a hot leaf" claim was based on the
   deltaShare column alone, which was inflated by a single anomalous
   inter-sample gap. The unweighted hitCount column shows cwi as a
   12-in-38582 statistical blip, not a hot leaf.
