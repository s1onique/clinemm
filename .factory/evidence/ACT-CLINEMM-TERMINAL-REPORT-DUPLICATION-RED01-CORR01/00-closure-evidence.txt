  the per-row marker contract.

  distinct source identity (msg_15LGa4py + call_function_xxakngsgiq8s_1
  for row A; msg_RagTZXRy + call_function_jyd622908p3h_1 for row B).
  No duplication in the message collection.

  The two rows are distinct historical entities, the renderer
  explains both, the per-marker test (terminalReportFraming.ts)
  treats both as "Completed". The test ALONE does not establish
  the chat-surface contract — the chat-surface contract is the
  user's expectation of "one task = one completed report".

consistent and the engineering seam is verified, but the documented

"intentional history" of two distinct historical submissions, is a

6. Causal boundary and product-contract question
============================================================

Stop at the first proven causal boundary:

  The two visible CompletionOutputRow cards on reopen are the
  direct, mechanical consequence of the restoration chain
  processing the two submit_and_exit tool_use blocks in the
  persisted transcript. The chain is documented and tested
  (TRD01). The cards are distinct per the per-row marker
  system. The "Completed" badge is correct per row.

The product question that exceeds this ACT's scope:

  When a task has multiple historical submit_and_exit tool_uses
  (legitimate in the BCB-held-then-released scenario), should
  the chat surface show one "Completed" card (the latest) or N
  cards (one per submit_and_exit)?

This is a product-contract decision that needs an explicit ruling
from product, not an engineering inference. The current
implementation shows N. Engineering seam: verified.
