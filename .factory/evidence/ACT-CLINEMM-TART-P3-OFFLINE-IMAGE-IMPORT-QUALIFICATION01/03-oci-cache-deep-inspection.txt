=== Conclusion (per ACT §2.5 'Important distinction') ===

  OCI cache entry (visible in `tart list --source oci`) is NOT equivalent
  to a local VM, and is NOT equivalent to a .tvm archive on disk.

  Specifically:
    * The 1.1 MB cache footprint proves the OCI image body has NOT been
      fully cached on this host.
    * Re-pulling the body requires a registry credential and ~800 MB of
      network egress, AND would write the OCI cache DB back to the
      protect volume (where P2 proved SQLite WAL fails with
      'unhandled result=8').
    * Even on success, `tart pull` materializes a local VM into
      TART_HOME/VirtualMachines; it does NOT produce a .tvm file. The
      only path to a .tvm is `tart export <local-vm>`, which requires
      a local VM to exist first.

  No .tvm can be produced from this Seatbelted ClineMM task.
