ClineMM peculiarity recorded
-----------------------------
   ClineMM does NOT implement the upstream three-level progressive
   disclosure (L1 metadata / L2 SKILL.md body / L3 resources). It
   collapses L2 + L3: `SkillConfig.instructions` is the entire
   single-pass body of SKILL.md. There is no `resources:` field, no
   skill-root URI, no auto-discovery of sub-files. Resource references
   appear as literal text in the body and must be loaded by generic
   file tools. SW-CM03 honors this by asserting on body-text-reference
   (path appears) AND on no-body-inlining (resource contents absent).
