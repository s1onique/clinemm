TERMINATION WINDOW ESCAPE
==========================

This ACT is a discriminator of OBSERVATION primitives. Termination
is performed by the helper's existing kill(-pgid, SIGTERM) ->
SIGKILL escalation, NOT by candidate A/B/C themselves.

The termination-window-escape test (a process forks during the
SIGTERM grace period, in the hope that the new descendant is not
terminated) is an interesting adversarial case for any containment
primitive, but it is OUT OF SCOPE for this ACT:

  - The discriminator only proves whether a primitive CAN OBSERVE
    the descendant set. It does not change the termination
    authority, which remains helper-owned PGID signal.
  - With candidate A or B selected, the question would become
    "does the chosen observation primitive continue to observe
    during the SIGTERM grace window?" That is an integration
    question, not a discriminator question.

If/when a production wiring ACT is authorized (which this ACT
defers — selection is NONE), termination-window tests become
appropriate.

TERMINATION_WINDOW_ESCAPE = NOT_EXERCISED
    (deferred to a production-wiring ACT, if any)
