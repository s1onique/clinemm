### Why AUTO_MERGE = 38, not 37 (arithmetic correction)

The earlier recon draft computed `AUTO_MERGE = 54 - 17 = 37`, which is wrong
because the add/add test is **double-subtracted**: it is in INTERSECTION
(55) but NOT in CHANGED_IN_BOTH (54), so subtracting all 17 conflicts from
the 54-set removes it zero times and from the 55-set removes it once.

The correct accounting decomposes the 17 conflicts into:

```
BASE_PRESENT_CONFLICTS = 16   (CONFLICT (content) entries — these are in
                              CHANGED_IN_BOTH)
ADD_ADD_CONFLICTS      = 1    (useProviderUsageCostDisplay.test.ts — this is
                              in INTERSECTION but not in CHANGED_IN_BOTH)
TOTAL                  = 17
```

And the auto-merge count is the same by either path:

```
AUTO_MERGE = INTERSECTION - TOTAL_CONFLICTS
           = 55 - 17
           = 38

AUTO_MERGE = CHANGED_IN_BOTH - BASE_PRESENT_CONFLICTS
           = 54 - 16
           = 38
```

This does not block the merge; the 17 real conflicts come directly from
merge-tree regardless.
