## Negative confirmations

```text
- No production caller of commandJobManager.cancel({ jobId }) exists
  outside of vscode-session-host.ts:506.
- No production caller of commandJobManager.dispose() exists outside of
  vscode-session-host.ts:592.
- No production caller of commandJobManager.terminate() exists outside of
  the manager itself (deadline, abortSignal listener, dispose).
- No production caller of VscodeSessionHost.dispose() exists outside of
  sdk-session-lifecycle.ts:501.
```

## Conclusion

The cancellation requester for the LIVE job MUST be one of:

```text
(a) UiCancelRpc      — webview background-cancel button path A
(b) CallerAbortSignal — caller's AbortSignal aborted (path D)
(c) ExtensionShutdown — SdkController.dispose (path F) → host.dispose → manager.dispose
```

The LIVE evidence has `job_active_removed.reason = "cancel"`, so (b), (a),
and (c) are the only candidates. CP1 (UiCancelRpc) is the most likely
because (b) has no production caller for the background path and (c) is
the controller-shutdown path which would also kill the manager (replacing
M2 with nothing, not adding a manager_dispose_begin to a still-present
manager). The next ACT will instrument the request boundary of each of
these three seams with an explicit caller identity so the LIVE dump can
classify deterministically.
