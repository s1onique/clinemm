## What the bounded defect IS

```text
Property: at the moment the foreground tool returns RUNNING (line 709),
          the caller AbortSignal listener remains attached to context.signal.

Effect:   any later caller-supplied abort fires the listener, which calls
          manager.terminate(job, "cancel") with origin="caller_abort_signal".

Live:     ACT-CLINEMM-BACKGROUND-COMMAND-CANCELLATION-PROVENANCE01 captured
          exactly this on the dogfood VSIX — requestOrigin="caller_abort_signal"
          on the detached job, with operator having done nothing.
```
