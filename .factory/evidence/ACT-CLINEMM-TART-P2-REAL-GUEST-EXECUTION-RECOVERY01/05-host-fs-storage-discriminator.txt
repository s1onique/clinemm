TIME_MACHINE_SNAPSHOT_CAUSED_DENIAL:                    INFERRED (not the proximate cause)
  The snapshots at /private/tmp/snapshot are READ-ONLY
  MOUNTS of /dev/disk3s8; they do not by themselves
  make /dev/disk3s8 read-only. The /dev/disk3s8 volume
  carries the APFS 'protect' mount flag independently.
  The actual mechanism (per the expert's analysis) is the
  APFS 'protect' flag on /dev/disk3s8, which blocks
  utimes(2) and SQLite WAL writes regardless of any
  snapshot presence.
REBOOT_WILL_FIX_IT:                                    NOT PROVEN
  Reboot would unmount /dev/disk3s8 and potentially
  clear the 'protect' flag if it was applied by a
  session-level mechanism (e.g. macOS data volume
  protection on Time Machine snapshot mount). But the
  flag may also be a persistent APFS volume attribute
  that survives reboot. Cannot conclude from the
  available evidence.

PARTIAL: /private/tmp CANNOT host the OCI cache
  - The OCI cache is at ~/Library/Caches/tart/ which
    uses getpwuid()->pw_dir, NOT $HOME env var
  - On this host, getpwuid()->pw_dir = /Volumes/UserData/Users/chistyakov

  - tart clone therefore cannot re-pull or re-validate
