  CORRECTION02 fixed the previous P0 (seq-domain identity), but

  TurnState-seq on EVERY accepted observation, yet the selector
  interprets that stamp as bound to the shadow's PROJECTED PHASE.
  A noop shadow observation (compareWith-shadow-adapter.ts:212
  in the agents package) under a fresh TurnState generation that
  leaves the shadow projection unchanged advances the stamp
  without changing the projected phase — the LIVE contradiction
  resurfaces.

PURPOSE OF THIS CORRECTION
  Bounded single-axis correction. Stamp the comparator's
  TurnState-seq on a PHASE-KEYED basis:

    lastObservedTurnSeq: Map<TurnPhase, number | undefined>

  Updated by `compareWith` ONLY when the observation event is
  NOT a `"noop"` sentinel — every non-noop observation that
  yields shadowPhase = P updates the entry
  `lastObservedTurnSeq[P] = turnSeq`. A noop observation does
  not update any entry.

  Now the selector's gate compares:

    publication.seq > lastObservedTurnSeqForPhase(canonicalShadowPhase)

  i.e. "is the legacy's current generation newer than the
  generation under which the PHASE THE SHADOW IS PROJECTING was
  last established?" — the per-projection invariant the
  reviewer demanded.
