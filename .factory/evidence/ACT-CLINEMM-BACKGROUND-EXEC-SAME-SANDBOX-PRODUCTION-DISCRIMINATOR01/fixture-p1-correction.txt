ACT:      ACT-CLINEMM-BACKGROUND-EXEC-SAME-SANDBOX-PRODUCTION-DISCRIMINATOR01
SOURCE:   review of 2026-11-05 (P1 finding: PGID inspection can race the
          child's four-second lifetime)
CLASSIFICATION: STRUCTURAL (fixture-only correction)

--- THE FINDING ---
The diagnostic fixture performed TWO synchronous `/bin/ps` observations in
the PRE-SIGNAL path (one for the parent's PGID, one for the child's). Each
had a 2-second timeout. The child has an independent 4-second self-exit
deadline.

Worst case: 2 x 2000ms = 4000ms = the child's ENTIRE lifetime.
=> The SIGTERM could be attempted only AFTER the child had already exited,
   yielding an inconclusive ESRCH that cannot discriminate
   SELECT_SEATBELT_SIGNAL_REPAIR from SELECT_COMPOSED_BOUNDARY_REPAIR.

This is a REAL production risk: the generated profile contains
`(allow process-exec)`, so `/bin/ps` may be PERMITTED there. (It is only
*denied* in the agent's own substrate, where the failure is fast.)

--- THE FIX (applied once) ---
Removed BOTH PGID observations from the pre-signal path:
  - deleted the `observePgid()` function
  - deleted the `execFileSync` import
  - deleted `result.childPgid = observePgid(childPid);`
  - `parentPgid` and `childPgid` are now always `null`
The authoritative PGID inspection is delegated to the independent Terminal
(OPERATOR-INSTRUCTIONS.md step 5).
