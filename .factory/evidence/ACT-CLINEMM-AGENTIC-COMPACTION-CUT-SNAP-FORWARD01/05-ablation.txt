ABLATION
================================================

The RED cases already pin the necessity of the fix. The patch is a one-clause
inversion. Per the ACT charter §18 "Strong ablations", we must prove
necessity for the chosen class.

The fix is necessary because:

1. RED R1 (L1 long tool loop) FAILS pre-patch on the actual production code path.
   The cut returned by findCutIndex on a 472-message live-shape fixture is 1
   (collapsed by snap-clamp). The required cut is ~451 (token-budget walk
   value). Difference: 450 messages reintroduced into the tail.

2. RED R5 (L1 fold size) FAILS pre-patch: foldSize=1 << required foldSize>=400.

3. Forward snap-walk is the right primitive: when candidate lands inside a
   tool_result (unsafe), the old backward walk would walk back into the tool_use
   (potentially collapsing the fold); the new forward walk goes past the
   tool_result to the next safe boundary (the next assistant or typed-user).
   In L1's case, candidate=451 is already on a safe boundary (assistant tool_use)
   so forward walk returns immediately with cut=451.

4. The fallback backward walk (when forward runs off the tail) is the same
   atomicity-preserving logic as the OLD code — it just isn't the primary path.
