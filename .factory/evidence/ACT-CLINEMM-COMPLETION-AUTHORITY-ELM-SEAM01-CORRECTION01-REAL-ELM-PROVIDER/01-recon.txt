COMMIT_RECORD_SEMANTICS:
  stage=task_completion_committed is a POST-DECISION factual record.
  Telling the authority kernel that the commit happened before the gate
  asks 'may commit happen?' is a self-fulfilling loop. The runtime
  enforces chronology discipline at the enqueue seam (AUTHORITY_STAGES
  set): task_completion_committed / completion_presented / task_cancelled
  are silently dropped before reaching the authority kernel.

SAFE_PRE_EFFECT_ELM_STATE:
  All CCARD stages EXCEPT task_completion_committed, completion_presented,
  task_cancelled. These are the pre-effect facts Elm knows about.

CURRENT_ELM_DECISION_OUTPUT:
  The Elm kernel already owns the closed HoldReason enum and the
  AuthorizeTaskCompletion effect. Per Authority.elm line 599-610
  completionAuthorized = List.isEmpty reasons. The decision is
  explicit in Elm. No TS reimplementation of computeHoldReasons.

SHADOW_SYNC_STATUS:
  Shadow continues fire-and-forget (setTimeout(0) per inbound, observable
  from a separate microtask). Authority MUST NOT depend on the shadow's
  async ring; it owns its own per-session kernel instance.

AUTHORITY_SYNC_PATH:
  Per-session queue (Map<sessionId, Record[]>) in
  completion-authority-elm-authority-runtime.ts. Records enqueued
  synchronously at the CCARD capture site. Gate awaits one microtask
  per record to drain the Elm outbound queue. Decision is read
  synchronously from lastDecision after the flush.
