C3 SPLIT — IP-timeout and SSH-timeout are distinct error codes

Date: 2026-10-08
Subject HEAD: d46223b51

The lifecycle in handle_tart_testbed_run (tools/macos-host-helper/native/helper.c)
splits WAIT_IP and WAIT_SSH into two bounded phases:

  1. tbr_wait_ip(tart_path, vm, 60s deadline, 1s poll) — bounded
     polling of `tart ip`. Returns 0 with ip_out populated on
     success; 1 on timeout; 2 on Tart resolution failure.
     Primary failure: TART_IP_TIMEOUT (1) or TART_START_FAILED (2).

  2. tbr_wait_ssh(ssh_path, kh, id, user, ip, 30s deadline, 1s poll)
     — bounded polling of `ssh <ip> true`. Returns 0 on success;
     1 on timeout; 2 on env failure. Primary failure:
     TART_SSH_TIMEOUT (1) or TESTBED_PREPARE_FAILED (2).

The split is required by the brief: "Required failure: TART_SSH_TIMEOUT,
not TART_COPY_IN_FAILED." The helper no longer collapses both into
TART_COPY_IN_FAILED.

VERIFICATION (TBRUNNER-C-09-1, TBRUNNER-C-01, TBRUNNER-C-04):
  With fake-tart returning IP=192.168.64.42, the lifecycle advances
  to WAIT_SSH and times out with TART_SSH_TIMEOUT (not TART_IP_TIMEOUT,
  not TART_COPY_IN_FAILED). 10 tests pass, 0 fail.

  Test C-09-1: "SSH unavailable -> TART_SSH_TIMEOUT (not TART_IP_TIMEOUT)"
