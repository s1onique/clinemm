              - BASH_ENV startup file can define `mktemp()`

              (/usr/bin/mktemp) UNRELATED to what the executor
              actually invokes.

                /usr/bin/mktemp
                /usr/bin/mktemp -d

              source label host_mktemp_shell_resolution_unbound
              documenting the shell-resolution gap. UX trade-off:

              explicit path for AUTO. The mktemp saga is genuinely
              closed at the policy layer once this lands.
