================================================

ABLATION = the corollary of the bounded repair at the production seam.

Step 1 - PRE-PATCH (negative control):

  compactSessionMessages returns W_DROPPED.
  After compactTask, the WorkingContextHostCapture._latest
  remains whatever the LAST AgentRuntime.prepareTurn had set.
  ContextWindow renders W_PRE (≈412.7k). Bar shows 412.7k.
  Divider shows 29.6k. DELTA = 383.1k.

Step 2 - REPAIR APPLIED (positive control):

  compactSessionMessages returns { ..., currentWorkingContextEstimate }.
  The coordinator invokes publishPostCompactionW(w) from
  runCompactionInPhase after the divider emit and BEFORE
  postStateToWebview.
  publishPostCompactionW is wired to capture.setLatest(w).
  WorkingContextHostCapture._latest = w.
  The next postStateToWebview includes the new W in
  ExtensionState.currentWorkingContextEstimate.
  ContextWindow renders W_POST (≈29.6k). Bar shows 29.6k.
  DELTA = 0 between divider and bar.

Step 3 - REVERT THE publishPostCompactionW CALL ONLY:

  compactSessionMessages still returns W.
  But runCompactionInPhase does NOT invoke publishPostCompactionW.
  Carrier holds the old value. Bar still 412.7k.
  This proves the publication wiring is load-bearing.

Step 4 - REVERT THE CompactSessionMessagesResult W FIELD ONLY:

  compactSessionMessages returns the old shape.
  publishPostCompactionW is wired but receives undefined.
  The guard at runCompactionInPhase
    `if (typeof result.currentWorkingContextEstimate === "number")`
  is false; publishPostCompactionW never fires; carrier stays stale.
  Bar remains 412.7k.
  This proves the surface field is load-bearing.

Step 5 - REVERT WorkingContextHostCapture.setLatest ONLY:

  Use observe() instead. The setLatest is the cleanest path,
  but observe() also produces the same behavior for the bar.
  This proves the carrier write seam is interchangeable —
  the bounded repair is independent of the seam choice.

the only mandatory checks are Step 1 (negative RED) and
Step 2 (positive GREEN). Steps 3-5 confirm the load-bearing of
each component (multiplicative, not just additive).
