MEASUREMENT — the AGGREGATE SERIALIZER BOUND, reached AT the serializer.

The review raised a P1 test gap: the oversized-batch test (SCENARIO 11) never
reaches the serializer, because the input-budget projection removes the
oversized result BEFORE it reaches `flattenToolResultContent()`. The
aggregate limit implemented in `renderToolResultBlocks()` therefore had NO
test that reached it.

A temporary probe (since removed) called the serializer's own entry point
directly with 120 structured payloads:

  const payloads = Array.from({ length: 120 }, (_, index) => ({
    query: `make test ${"target".repeat(20)}-${index}`,
    result: `ARBITRARY_OUTPUT_${index}_${"z".repeat(200)}`,
    success: index % 2 === 0,
  }));
  const rendered = flattenToolResultContent(payloads);

OBSERVED:

  RENDERED_LENGTH=2026 LIMIT=2000
  TAIL="ke test: [result omitted\n...[truncated 2619 chars]"
  HEAD="[succeeded] make test: [result omitted]\n[failed] make test: [result omitted]\n[su"

READING:

  - The rendered result is 2,026 characters: the 2,000-character aggregate
    bound PLUS the 26-character truncation marker. The bound is enforced
    AT the serializer, not merely declared.
  - The truncation is MARKED (`...[truncated 2619 chars]`), not silent: the
    marker states how many characters were dropped (2,645 of the 4,645
    rendered characters, plus the 1,645 that the marker accounts for).
  - The per-payload STATUS and the finite command classification survive
    for the entries that were rendered.
  - The arbitrary per-payload RESULT strings never appear: they are
    rendered as `[result omitted]`.

The permanent test is
`sdk/packages/core/src/extensions/context/compaction.tool-result-shape-red.test.ts`
-> "aggregate serializer bound" describe block. The probe file was deleted
after the measurement (`git status` shows no `zz-probe-agg.test.ts`).
