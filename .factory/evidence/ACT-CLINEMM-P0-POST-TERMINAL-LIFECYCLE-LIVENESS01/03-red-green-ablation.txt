conserved (LCV-11).

## 3.4 R3 — causal necessity (minimal ablation at the real production boundary)

### L1 necessity

The barrier re-evaluation is driven from exactly TWO non-test call sites:

```
$ grep -n 'reevaluateDeferredCompletionBarrier()' src/sdk/*.ts | grep -v test
sdk-session-event-coordinator.ts:1649   # inside notifyAgentTurnDone
SdkController.ts:5458                   # inside maybeReevaluateDeferredContinuation
```

Neither is the BCB obligation **drain**. RED-03 constructs the exact
production chronology — five held obligations drained through the production
Path C observation seam (`consumeNonNotifyTerminalObservation`, the canonical
`command_status` caller), Elm HOLD on `scheduled_continuation`, then that
dependency cleared with **no** further `agent_turn_done` and **no**
background-command-state transition — and C10 is never attempted.

Ablation (bounded, at the real boundary): adding a single
`reevaluateDeferredCompletionBarrier()` call at the moment the LAST held
obligation drains turns RED-03 GREEN with **zero** change to any guard, to the
Elm gate, or to the conservation predicates. That is the minimal demonstration
that the missing causal re-evaluation — and nothing else — is responsible.

**Guard-bypass explicitly NOT used as the repair:** forcing `completed` when
the runtime goes idle, bypassing the Elm HOLD, clearing BCB obligations without
observation, or adding a retry loop/timer would all violate the safety
criterion. The repair adds a trigger on the real causal event only.

### L2 necessity

`sdk-task-control-coordinator.ts:100-111` early-returns on
`!activeSession` at a point where `raiseCancelFence`, `setTurnPhase` and
`postStateToWebview` are all downstream. Ablation: moving the
no-active-session branch to the END of `cancelTask` (so it still skips the
abort but runs the fence + phase + publish tail) turns RED-04 and LCV-09
GREEN without touching the active-session path — proving the early return
alone is responsible.

Reachability is REAL, not method-call-only:
`ActionButtons(secondaryAction === "cancel")` →
`useMessageHandlers.ts:583` → `TaskServiceClient.cancelTask` (gRPC) →
`src/core/controller/task/cancelTask.ts` → `controller.cancelTask()` →
`SdkTaskControlCoordinator.cancelTask()`. The existing DEFAULT_OFF
`cancel-liveness-diagnostic` already names this exact signature
`H-A = (req=1, acc=0, inv=0, cmp=0, pub=0)`.
