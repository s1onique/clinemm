Why the 100x kqueue race hammer was not run
(verbatim from 84-run3-adjudication.txt):

  100X_RACE_HAMMER = NOT_RUN
  WHY_NOT_RUN      = primitive already falsified; additional
                     repetitions cannot restore a universal
                     containment claim.

The single fixture-G witness is stronger than a 100-iteration hammer
for one specific reason: fixture G's race window is construction-fixed.
The grandchild fork happens immediately after setsid() returns; this
sequence is the kernel's own ordering, not a probabilistic outcome.
A hammer would only add the same (pid,start_us) pair 100 more times
without strengthening or weakening the falsification.

The oracle-correction rounds (round-1 through round-8) of the
predecessor ACT produced their only effect: making the negative result
trustworthy. None of those rounds changed the production classifier
itself; they repaired the oracle so the (missed_ground_truth_count,
unique_missed_identities) pair is verifiable. Do not reopen them.
