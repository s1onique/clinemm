Closed facts inherited from predecessor ACTs and bound by the
operator captures copied into 80-/81-/82-/83-/84-*.{txt,jsonl}.

PRIMARY_PGID_CLEANUP
  = PROVEN for inherited-PGID trees
  source: ACT-CLINEMM-HOST-HELPER-OWNED-PGID-TERMINATION01 +
          ACT-CLINEMM-COMMANDJOB-DESCENDANT-CONSERVATION-TELEMETRY01
  evidence: production CommandJobManager terminates the leader PGID
            with SIGTERM -> grace -> SIGKILL; ordinary subprocess
            children (no setsid, no detached:true) are reaped.

B_suspended_preattach_kqueue
  = REFUTED by RUN_3 fixture G
  source: 82-run3-double-fork-setsid-G.jsonl
  reason:  even with POSIX_SPAWN_START_SUSPENDED and the watch
           registered before SIGCONT, the second fork after setsid
           in the child is not observable as a NOTE_FORK on the
           parent's kqueue in time to register the grandchild.

C_endpoint_security_descendants
  = unavailable on CURRENT Sonoma/SDK/signing substrate
  source: this ACT's recon (see 30-strategy-c-platform.txt,
          31-strategy-c-signing.txt, 32-strategy-c-entitlement.md)
  reason:  es_new_descendants_client() is NOT in the public SDK
           headers shipped with macOS 14.0 (the Sonoma SDK);
           es_new_client() returns ES_NEW_CLIENT_RESULT_ERR_NOT_ENTITLED
           for an adhoc-signed helper; the helper does not hold the
           com.apple.developer.endpoint-security.client entitlement.

GENERAL_DESCENDANT_CONTAINMENT
  = NOT IMPLEMENTED
  source: this conclusion
  reason:  no proven primitive is available on this substrate for
           universal descendant containment; the only remaining
           contracts are (a) prohibit session-detach (which requires
           a kernel seam that does not exist here) or (b) accept
           PGID-only containment and document the boundary.

This ACT does NOT re-litigate any of the above.
